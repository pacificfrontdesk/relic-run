Support

Relic Run · Puzzle Expedition

Get in touch

Email relicrun@pacificfrontdesk.com. One person reads it, so a reply usually takes a day or two rather than an hour.

It helps to include your device, your iOS version, and the level number shown at the top right of the screen.

Common questions

I am stuck on a board

Every board can be solved from what you can see, and the game never hides the answer behind a fixed order. Tap Hint and it will light a tile whose path is genuinely clear right now. The first hint on each board is free; after that it costs a compass, which you can buy at Supplies.

A cracked tile keeps crumbling

The number on a cracked tile counts down by one every time any tile leaves the board, not just when you touch that one. It has to be freed within that many moves, so clear the tiles blocking its path first and save the rest of the board for afterwards.

I lost my coins in a vault

A timed vault collapses when the clock runs out, and the coins you were carrying are lost with it. Relic fragments always survive, so collection progress is never at risk. A rope, bought at Supplies, saves the coins once.

The game got easier or harder on its own

That is deliberate. Boards adapt to how you are doing, giving more spare moves when you are struggling and fewer when you are not. You can see what it is currently doing, and override it, under Settings, Difficulty. Levels and rewards are never changed by it.

I lost my progress

Progress is kept on your device. Deleting the app, resetting the device, or moving to a new phone without a backup will clear it, and we cannot restore it, because there is no account and no server holding a copy.

The game is silent

Check the silent switch first, since the game deliberately respects it. Then open Settings in the app, where Sound effects and Ambience can each be turned off separately.

Does it need a connection?

No. Every level is generated and verified on your device, and the whole game works offline.
