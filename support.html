Support

Relic Run · Puzzle Expedition

Get in touch

Email support+relicrun@pacificfrontdesk.com. One person reads it, so a reply usually takes a day or two rather than an hour.

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the level number shown at the top right of the screen.

Common questions

I am stuck on a board

Every board can be solved from what you can see, and the game never hides the answer behind a fixed order. Tap Hint and it will light a tile whose path is genuinely clear right now. The first hint on each board is free; after that it uses a compass, which you can buy with coins in Supplies.

A cracked tile keeps crumbling

The number on a cracked tile counts down by one every time any tile leaves the board, not just when you touch that one. It has to be freed within that many moves, so clear the tiles blocking its path first and save the rest of the board for afterwards.

I lost my coins in a vault

A timed vault collapses when the clock runs out, and the coins you were carrying are lost with it. Relic fragments always survive, so collection progress is never at risk. A rope, bought with coins in Supplies, saves the coins once.

I am out of energy

A safe vault costs 2 energy and a risky one 4. The bar holds 30 and refills one point every ten minutes, so a full bar takes five hours. In Supplies, Rest gives 8 energy for free once an hour. If you would rather not wait, 10 gems refill the whole bar.

What do gems buy?

Energy refills - that is all. Tools (compass, pickaxe, rope) and the trader's relic fragments are bought with coins you win in vaults. Gem packs and the one-time Starter Pack are in Supplies, and each shows the App Store's own price on its button.

I bought something and did not get it

Purchases are delivered when the App Store confirms them, and a purchase that was interrupted is delivered the next time you open the game. If something is still missing, open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases: it re-grants the Starter Pack to the Apple Account that bought it. Gem packs are used up when they are delivered, so they cannot be restored; if gems you paid for never arrived, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are taken by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple Account you paid with. We cannot see your payment details or issue refunds ourselves.

The game got easier or harder on its own

That is deliberate. Boards adapt to how you are doing, giving more spare moves when you are struggling and fewer when you are not. You can see what it is currently doing, and override it, under Settings, Difficulty. Levels and rewards are never changed by it.

I lost my progress

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress is copied to your own iCloud account. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore from iCloud. It will tell you if this iPhone is already the furthest along.

If you were not signed in to iCloud, the save existed only on that iPhone and we cannot recover it, because there is no account and no server of ours holding a copy.

A new iPhone, or two iPhones, show different progress

Progress syncs through iCloud, and the save that has completed more puzzles always wins, so playing on the phone that is behind can never wipe out the one that is ahead. If they have not caught up, open the app on both while they are online, and use Restore from iCloud in Settings on the one that is behind.

Anything still in your bag from an unfinished expedition is not synced. It banks when the expedition ends.

The game is silent, or too loud

Check the silent switch first, since the game deliberately respects it. Then open Settings in the app, where Sound effects, Ambience and Music can each be turned off separately.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game reminds you when energy is full, when the trader restocks and when your daily streak is about to lapse. They are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent from a server. Turn them off in the game's Settings or in the iPhone Settings app.

Does it need a connection?

No. Every level is generated and verified on your iPhone, and the whole game works offline. Only purchases and iCloud syncing need a connection.
