Privacy Policy

Relic Run · last updated 20 October 2026

Who we are

Relic Run is published by Oleg Nochka, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Oleg Nochka is the person responsible for the app and for this policy.

The short version

Relic Run collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the game sends nothing to us. Your progress is saved on your iPhone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, copied into your own iCloud account so it survives a new phone. We never see it. This matches the app's App Store privacy label: "Data Not Collected".

Your progress

The game saves the following:

  • Your level, coins, gems, energy, tools, relics and museum progress
  • Your daily streak and expedition history
  • Your settings, including difficulty, sound, ambience, music, haptics and notifications
  • A short record of your recent puzzles, used on your iPhone to adjust how forgiving boards are
  • Whether you have bought the one-time Starter Pack

This is stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it from that iPhone.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the same progress is also written to your Apple Account, not to any computer of ours. We cannot read it, we are not told that it exists, and no part of it is ever sent to us. Apple handles the syncing under Apple's Privacy Policy.

It is a small file, a few kilobytes, holding only the progress listed above. It contains no name, no email address and nothing you have typed.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, the game simply saves on the iPhone and nothing is copied anywhere. To stop syncing at any time, sign out of iCloud or turn iCloud off for Relic Run in the Settings app. A copy already in your iCloud account stays there under your control, and is removed the way any other iCloud app data is, through Apple's settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are made through Apple. Apple takes the payment and tells the game only that a purchase succeeded, so the gems or the Starter Pack can be delivered. We never receive your name, email, card or Apple Account details. Apple sends us sales reports - how many of each item were sold, in which country, and the proceeds - which are totals, not records about you.

Statistics from Apple

Like every App Store developer, we can see figures that Apple compiles: downloads, App Store page views, sales and, only from people who have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, crash reports and anonymous usage totals. These come to us as aggregate numbers and do not identify anyone. The app itself contains no analytics software.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your iPhone (energy full, trader restocked, streak about to lapse). Nothing is sent from a server, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or the iPhone Settings app.

Advertising

There is no advertising in the game and no advertising software in it. We may advertise Relic Run itself on the App Store through Apple Ads; Apple reports only aggregate results to us, such as how many people saw or tapped an ad.

Children

Relic Run is suitable for children. Because it collects nothing personal, there is nothing personal to collect from a child either. There is no chat, no social feature and no way for one player to contact another. Purchases follow Apple's rules, including Ask to Buy for family accounts.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes the save on that iPhone. Signing out of iCloud, or turning iCloud off for Relic Run, stops the syncing. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete; if you have a question, email us.

Changes

If we ever add anything that collects data, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than buried.
